# 🎬 CineVault

A personal offline movie and series library for organizing, discovering and sharing your favorite content.

CineVault helps you save movie and series information directly on your device, so you can access your collection anytime — even without internet access.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Offline Movie Library
- Save movies and series locally on your device
- Access your collection without internet
- Manage your personal watch library

### 🔎 Smart Search
- Search movies and series
- Automatically fetch information
- View metadata including:
  - Title
  - Genre
  - Release date
  - Description
  - Cast information

### 🔗 Episode & Series Management
- Save complete series using season links
- Automatically organize episodes
- Manage seasons easily

### 📝 Personal Notes & Voice Memories
Add your own:
- Text notes
- Voice messages

to specific moments of a movie.

When sharing your movie link, your friends can see your notes and hear your voice messages at the exact timestamp.

### 📺 Casting Support
Watch your content on compatible TVs using casting features.

### 📊 Personal Statistics
Understand your watching habits:
- Favorite genres
- Viewing patterns
- Personal movie statistics

### 🤝 Sharing
Share your movie collections and recommendations with friends.

---

## 🚀 Why CineVault?

Streaming platforms are great, but sometimes you don't have:
- Internet access
- Mobile signal
- Your saved recommendations available

CineVault gives you your own personal movie database.

## CineVault چیست؟

CineVault یک کتابخانه شخصی فیلم و سریال است که اطلاعات فیلم‌ها را روی دستگاه خود شما ذخیره می‌کند.

هدف این پروژه این است که بدون نیاز به اینترنت بتوانید:

- فیلم‌های مورد علاقه خود را مدیریت کنید
- اطلاعات فیلم‌ها را ببینید
- به دوستان خود معرفی کنید
- یادداشت و ویس روی لحظات خاص فیلم قرار دهید
